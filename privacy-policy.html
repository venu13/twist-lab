Twist Lab privacy policy

Published by Venu (individual developer)

Applies to the Android app “Twist Lab” (package com.venu.twistlab). Effective 17 October 2026.

In short: Twist Lab has no account, no server, no analytics and no ads. What you do in the app stays on your phone. The developer receives no data from you.

What the app stores, and where

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only on your device, in its private app storage:

  • which problems you marked as solved by yourself, and which guides and cards you ticked;
  • your mock interview results and your notes from mock rounds;
  • the behavioral stories you write, with their tags;
  • your study-plan date and hours per day, and your plan ticks.

The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, microphone or camera, and it does not request any of those permissions. It does not request the Internet permission at all.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The app has no server to send data to, no analytics or crash-reporting library, no advertising, and no third-party code that collects data.

Exporting and importing your data

You can export your progress, stories, plan and mock results to a single file. This only happens when you tap Export progress. The app hands the file to Android's share sheet, and you choose where it goes (for example, your own cloud drive, email or another app). The developer never sees it. When you import, the app reads only the file you pick, checks it, and shows you what will be replaced before anything changes.

Links to other websites

Some study cards and problems link to outside material (documentation, video search pages and practice problems on other sites). A link opens in your own web browser only when you tap it. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and the app does not send them any of your data.

Android backup

Like most apps, Twist Lab allows Android's own backup of its progress file. If you have Android backup turned on in your phone settings, Android may keep a copy in your Google account and restore it when you set up a new phone. That is controlled by your device and your Google account settings, not by the developer, who has no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's system settings.

Deleting your data

Use Reset progress on the Progress tab to erase it inside the app, or uninstall the app, which removes its stored data from your phone. Files you exported yourself are yours to delete wherever you saved them.

Children

Twist Lab is a study tool for adults preparing for job interviews and is not directed to children. Because it collects no personal information, it holds no information about children either.

Changes

If the app ever changes what it does with data, this page will be updated before the change ships and the date above will change.
